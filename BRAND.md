# Ofortuna Brand Guidelines

This guide explains how to present the Ofortuna brand consistently and professionally.

## NAMING

Ofortuna is a single word, always spelled with a capital “O”. It is the brand name of both the company and the application (not “Ofortuna app”). When referring to dedicated releases from Ofortuna, we ask that you capitalize them as proper nouns (e.g. Ofortuna Method”).

## USAGE

Provide plenty of space around Ofortuna assets. Make them big or make them small, but give them room to breathe. They shouldn’t feel cramped or cluttered.

## Ofortuna wordmark⁠

The Ofortuna wordmark should be used in all references to Ofortuna as space allows. Monochrome usage is preferred with the brand colors below.

## Ofortuna icon

When referring to Ofortuna as a company, such as on social media, or where a “chip” design is required, it is acceptable to use this stylized icon with an appropriate corner radius.
